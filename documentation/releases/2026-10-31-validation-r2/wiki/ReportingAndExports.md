# Reporting and exports

Reports belong to the loaded experiment, selected SOP and identified build. Keep original data alongside the exported interpretation. Pseudonymisation and filter settings affect what is exported; retain any re-identification material separately.

Exports and screenshots have their own byte identities. A changed font or embedded audio asset can alter a file hash without changing calculations. Hashes prove matching bytes, not scientific validity, calibration or security.

Training reports are artificial-data learning records. They must not be presented as laboratory result reports or professional certification.
