# SOP and results

Applicability is evaluated before sample interpretation. A profile outside its declared method scope is **Profile not applicable**, not a negative sample result. Controls, replicate rules and the reason for an outcome must remain visible.

Laboratory owners must approve method-specific thresholds, revisions, control-name mapping and acceptance rules. A supplied template or synthetic training profile is not an approved laboratory SOP.

MARA profile metadata and mathematical tests have separate evidence. The R2 definitions test checks 32 pinned shipped-library hashes and synthetic run outcomes, alongside mathematical tests. Original failures remain in the review logs.

Review-only profiles do not authorise release. Stored values, derived results and rule conclusions must be distinguishable.
