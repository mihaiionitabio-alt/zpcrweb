# Historical function evidence

These **705 symbol records** come from volume 4 of Zenodo record 23437639. They describe the historical harness: 676 included symbols plus 29 outside it. Constants are included; this count is not comparable to the current nested-function count. Historical “pass” can mean only that a call did not throw, not scientific correctness. The records are preserved, not relabelled as current checks.
