# MISSY Calm v1 — ten parts

Open index.html. Each standalone MP4 repeats the introduction and has chart pauses. The operator clips are byte-identical to the previous approved set. MISSY narration uses generic Microsoft Zira synthesis with neutral pitch, steady pace and sentence pauses. No television recording was used, cloned or embedded. Cq is spoken as threshold cycle. English and Chinese subtitle files are included.

These are actual webpage screenshot sequences, not continuous screen capture or a physical microphone test. Artificial data; not laboratory results. Existing readers, calculations, results and export code are unchanged.

MISSY embeds 194 English phrases, shared by the page and its built-in voice demonstration. Uncached dynamic answers require an installed local voice; the preset prefers Zira and uses neutral pitch and rate 0.9. Other operating systems may supply a different local voice. Chinese speech requires an installed local Chinese voice. The new audio bank has no network dependency. Speaker volume and subjective listening quality cannot be established by signal tests.
